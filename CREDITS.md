# Credits and third-party assets

Every asset in the game that someone else made, and what its licence requires.

**Add the entry the day you download something, not later.** The model is easy to find again; the URL it came from
is not, and a CC-BY credit without a link doesn't satisfy the licence.

This file needs to reach players, not just sit in the repo. Two places:

- an in-game credits screen (the proper answer)
- a `CREDITS.txt` shipped next to the .exe (the cheap answer, and enough on its own)

Steam's store page has a legal/about field worth pasting it into as well.

---

## Format

```
### "Model Name"
- Author: NAME
- Source: URL
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Modified: yes/no — say what changed
```

"Modified" matters. CC-BY requires you to state whether you altered the work, and re-materialling, rescaling or
splitting a mesh all count.
